## ⚡ Quickstart

Simply run the included batch file:
```cmd

```

### Option 2: PowerShell
```powershell

```

### Option 3: Python / uv
```bash
uv run python -X utf8 tarot.py
```
*(or with standard python if `rich` is installed: `python tarot.py`)*

## 🌟 What Makes This Never Built Before?

Most Tarot programs on the internet are simple static dictionaries that print a single line of text. **ARCANA: Veil of Destiny** transforms Tarot into a living, interactive CLI world:

1. **Complete 78-Card Deck with True Reversals (`▲` / `▼`)**:
   - All 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana (Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles).
   - Inverted cards are not just given negative keywords: their visual ASCII frames flip upside down, their glyphs invert, their warning banners shift to Eclipse Crimson, and their shadow counsels reveal subconscious blockages and hidden trials.
2. **Interactive Physical Rituals**:
   - **Animated Riffle Shuffles**: Experience the visual cards being cleansed and shuffled.
   - **Interactive Deck Cuts**: Divide the virtual deck at the upper (25%), middle (50%), or subterranean (75%) depth.
   - **The Turning Flip**: Cards appear face-down, rotate into mid-air, and blaze into full reveal accompanied by Solfeggio bell chimes.
3. **Four Distinct Game Modes**:
   - 🌌 **The Divination Sanctuary**: Perform authentic spreads (Single Mirror, 3-Card Trinity of Time, 5-Card Cross of Truth, 7-Card Horseshoe of Fate, and the revered 10-Card Celtic Cross). Includes terminal side-by-side card layouts, position maps, and a holistic **Cross-Card Elemental Dignities & Synthesis Engine**.
   - 📖 **The Fate Weaver RPG**: An atmospheric interactive text adventure across the 4 mystical realms. At every junction, your real card draws determine your trials, elemental boons, and branching endings (Sovereign of Dawn, Eclipse Master, Alchemical Weaver).
   - ⚔️ **The Duel of Mirrors**: A strategic Tarot combat minigame where you duel your Shadow Doppelganger. Upright cards unleash righteous strikes and celestial wards; Reversed cards cast forbidden hexes, siphons, and gambits. Wield your **Mirror Prism** to invert cards in your hand!
   - 🪐 **Celestial Alignment & Daily Oracle**: Discover your birth zodiac correspondence, planetary ruler, and draw your daily personal prophecy and mantra.
4. **Persistent Grimoire Journal**:
   - Every reading is inscribed into `readings_chronicle.json`.
   - Browse your past prophecies, inspect card distributions, or export readings into formatted Markdown (`.md`) files.
   - Search the entire 78-card Codex encyclopedia with both upright and shadow lore.
5. **Atmospheric Sensory Audio**:
   - Built-in harmonic chimes on Windows using Solfeggio frequencies (528Hz love/miracle tone, 660Hz clarity, 880Hz intuition, and deep shadow hums). Can be muted at any time in Sanctum Settings.
